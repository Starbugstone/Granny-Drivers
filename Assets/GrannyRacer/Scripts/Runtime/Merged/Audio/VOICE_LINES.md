# Granny voice line script

## Input

- Yes, yes, I heard you. I am old, not buffering.
- Stop mashing buttons. You will wake my arthritis.
- One button at a time, dear. This is not a piano.
- I am moving! These knees run on dial-up.
- Keep your bloomers on. I heard you the first time.

## Collision

- Oi! That is coming out of your inheritance.
- Mind the paintwork! And my hip.
- I have had softer landings in church pews.
- Who put that there? Probably a youngster.
- That dent was vintage!

## Item pickup

- Ooh, loot. Into the handbag it goes.
- Finders keepers. Granny's rules.
- Useful! Unlike half my grandchildren.
- I will keep this. You would only lose it.
- Pop that in the handbag, beside the emergency biscuits.

## Attack given

- Have a taste of pensioner power!
- Respect your elders. Preferably from the floor.
- That is for calling me slow!
- Handbag delivery. Express service!
- My bingo arm still works!

## Attack received

- Ow! I have had vaccinations gentler than that.
- You hit a grandmother? No birthday money for you.
- Cheeky little menace! Come here!
- My hip replacement has a better warranty than you.
- Right. You are off the Christmas card list.

The bundled audio uses the replacement MP3 voice set supplied by the project
owner on 2026-08-01. File IDs and Unity GUIDs are unchanged from the original
implementation, so existing gameplay and serialized references remain valid.

The production voice and generation-service licence must be confirmed before a
public release.
